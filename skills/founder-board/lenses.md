# The three lenses

The text each lens receives lives in `founder_vn.board` (`LENSES`); this file is the same text for the Facilitator. A lens is a published framework applied to the brief. The rule is to apply the framework and never speak as the author: no role-play, no invented quotes, no sentence put in the author's mouth.

| Lens | Framework applied | What it asks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offers | 100M Offers (Alex Hormozi) | Is the promise specific and measurable? Does the package make refusing foolish? Does the price match the value, not only the competitors? Which group clearly needs it and will pay? |
| Product | the Steve Jobs biography by Walter Isaacson | Is there one clear core experience? Does the business dare to say no to what dilutes it? Are the details, including unseen ones, cared for? Is the whole journey from first contact to return coherent? Would a customer tell others? |
| Systems | The E-Myth Revisited (Michael E. Gerber) | Would the business run if the owner left for a month? Is every repeated task written as a procedure? Could it be copied into a second site? Is the owner working in the business or on it? Is anyone watching the operating numbers? |

The Systems lens replaces the Monopoly lens of the upstream skill (Peter Thiel). The Monopoly question, what secret makes this business unique in a market of one, is the wrong question for a neighbourhood barbershop or café, where the risk is a kitchen or a chair that only the owner can run. The upstream rule is kept: the lenses summarise published frameworks and never speak as the author.

Each memo has the same fixed shape, in Vietnamese: score (1 to 10), vote (nên làm, làm có điều kiện, chưa nên làm), what is strong, what would kill the idea, open questions, conditions.
